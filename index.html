The short version

We didn't get the Watson St. place, but we got this one instead, and we're really happy about it! It's brand new, and the location is about as good as it gets for us. It's one bedroom smaller than Watson, the upstairs living room is on the small side, and the outside of the house itself isn't much to look at (the neighborhood around it is great, though). We have plans for all of that, and we put together some pictures to show you what we're thinking.

It's on two floors. Upstairs has the kitchen, a small living room, a dining area, and our bedroom. Downstairs there's another living room and two more bedrooms.

Where it is

It's about a six-minute walk to Davis Square along the bike path. That's far enough away that it's quiet, but close enough that we can walk to our favorite restaurants, the Somerville Theatre, the gym, and the T. It's also really close to where we live now. You can actually see it from our apartment, just across the bike path.

The breakfast nook

This is Holly's favorite part of the house. There's a bay window at the front, and we're definitely turning it into a breakfast nook, which will also be our main dining table. The plan is a built-in bench that wraps around the window, with a table in the middle. Holly is very excited about this one.

Upstairs

The kitchen is really nice, and everything is new. The living room is the tricky part. It's pretty small, and with the TV on one wall and windows on the others, there isn't a lot of room to work with. It's not a huge deal since there's a second living room downstairs, but we've been thinking about what to do with it (see the ideas below).
